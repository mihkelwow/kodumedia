# Code of conduct

KoduMedia's community is expected to be welcoming, constructive and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal paths or private
  media.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, security and project direction.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption and publishing another person's private information are not allowed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Serious incidents
should be reported privately to the maintainer rather than discussed publicly.

This policy applies in the repository and in project-related public or private
communication.
